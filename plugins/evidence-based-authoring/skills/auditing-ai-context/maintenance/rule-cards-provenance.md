# Rule-cards provenance map

Out-of-runtime maintainer record. The audit workflow does **not** pass this file to sub-agents — cards carry the rule and its numbers and no citation; this map carries the full citation with its locator, the origin, and the maintenance notes. Round-trip rule: a card plus its line here reconstruct everything known about the rule.

One line per card: `rule_id · source · origin-file · notes` (ownership / non-default `applies_to_target` / evidence caveat, where notable), then the line's record in the format of `../../scientific-method/references/record-format.md`. `group` is derivable from `rule_id`; `confidence`/evidence strength is the caveat in the notes. The `origin` g1–g8 group files were removed once carded — git history preserves them; the cards are now canonical. G10 and G11 have no group file: their origin is the section named on each line inside `research/2026-08-02_output-style-evidence.md`, and that note's three `Verification —` sections hold the quotes and numbers those cards copy. R-48 has no group file either: its line names its origin, a section of `research/2026-11-22_auditing-ai-context-evidence-update.md`.

**Status: G1–G11 complete — 104 cards; 7 source rules without cards (table at end).**

**Records.** A record says what the rule rests on: a norm with the document opened, the owner's decision, or a claim with its test, result and bounds. A claim stands as `supported` where an opened test entails it and an independent reader's verdict holds every rule of the method; a rule whose claim waits for such a test is its owner's decision, and its record names the claim as `H-<rule_id>` in `pending-evidence.md`. `citations.json` holds the words and figures that the lines of the two maps and of `pending-evidence.md` quote from their sources, one entry per quote; `python3 -I plugins/evidence-based-authoring/skills/scientific-method/maintenance/scripts/check_quotes.py --cache <dir> plugins/evidence-based-authoring/skills/auditing-ai-context/maintenance/citations.json`, run from the repository root, says for each whether the words stand in its source; an entry whose source is a PDF, a JSON answer or the output of a git command names in its `note` how to turn it into a text file. Run over the texts fetched on 2026-11-22, the command finds every quote in its source.
