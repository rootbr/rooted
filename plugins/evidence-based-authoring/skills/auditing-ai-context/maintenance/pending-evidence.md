# Hypotheses of the audit skill

The claims of this skill that no test backs yet. A claim about the skill's own checks, or a claim no rule rests on yet, is keyed `H-n`; a claim that a rule card, a gotcha or a section of the run set rests on is keyed `H-<id of its line>` and is named by that line's record in `rule-cards-provenance.md` or `skill-provenance.md`. Each names its measure and the outcome that would refute it; a hypothesis whose test is planned as our own also names its threshold, its runs and the file that holds the test, and is committed here before the test runs. A hypothesis that a test settles keeps its line, with the result and the new standing.
