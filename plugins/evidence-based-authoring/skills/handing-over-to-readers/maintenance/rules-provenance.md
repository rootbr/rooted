# Provenance of the handing-over-to-readers rules

One line per shipped rule of `references/rules.md`, keyed `R-n` by the operator's number, and one per bullet of its "What the research adds". Fields: the sources with their locators and the pointer to the quoted fragment · the formulation · the evidence class, the fit and whether the text was opened · the caveat. A source is `first-hand` when its text (abstract, HTML or full text, as the pointer says) was opened; a figure seen only in a search summary is `relay` and carries no rule. `README n` is entry n of the repository `README.md` §References, verified first-hand by the output-style programme. Classes and fit, recorded per rule in the research note `research/2026-10-21_reading-contract-evidence.md` § Rules and data: `research` (an opened `direct` or `transfer` study; a `weak` one is named only as consistent), `standard`, `practice` (the committed eval run, by eval name and expectation number; listed in `maintenance/pending-evidence.md` with the study it needs), `definition`, `card`. The formulation of every rule is the operator's list of 27 rules; a second formulation is named where one exists.
