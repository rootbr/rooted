# Hypotheses about the method

The claims about the method itself that no test backs yet, keyed `H-n`. Each names its measure and the outcome that would refute it; a hypothesis whose test is planned as our own also names its threshold, its runs and the file that holds the test, and is committed here before the test runs. A hypothesis that a test settles keeps its line, with the result and the new standing.

- **H-01** · the seven rules of the method are necessary and sufficient for admitting rules to this repository · kind: claim · standing: hypothesis · measure: the count of rule lines on which the seven rules give an admission the owner judges wrong, and the count of pairs of rules whose verdicts coincide on every line examined · against: none · scope: the rules of the skills of this repository, as their provenance maps record them · refuter: one rule line the owner judges bad that passes all seven rules, one he judges good that the rules keep out, or two rules whose verdicts coincide on every line examined

readers that are two instances of one model, Claude Opus 5.5 at effort xhigh, each in its own context with the same brief; the kinds of the method read with the reading rule of the protocol; rule lines shown to the readers without their records; each reader opened the brief and no other file, by the list of its tool calls in the harness transcript · fit: transfer · transfer: thirty rules of about a thousand were read in the run that counts; the readers had the reading rule of the protocol beside the definitions of the method; - **H-03** · two readers who work independently give the same standing to the same record of a claim about one event · kind: claim · standing: hypothesis · measure: the share of records of claims about one event to which both readers give the same standing · against: none · scope: records of claims about one event written by `references/method.md` and `references/record-format.md`, each read by two readers who work independently from those two files · refuter: the two readers give the same standing to fewer than four records in five
- **H-04** · a probe with a known direction catches a measure that follows surface form · kind: claim · standing: hypothesis · measure: the share of records whose measure follows surface form alone that fail the reader's question on the measure, question 3 of `../../agents/claim-skeptic.md` · against: the same records without the field `validity` · scope: records of claims resting on a test of our own, read by the plugin's `claim-skeptic` reader · refuter: records whose measure is known to follow surface form alone pass the question on the measure as often with the field as without it
