# Hypotheses about the method

The claims about the method itself that no test backs yet, keyed `H-n`. Each names its measure and the outcome that would refute it; a hypothesis whose test is planned as our own also names its threshold, its runs and the file that holds the test, and is committed here before the test runs. A hypothesis that a test settles keeps its line, with the result and the new standing.

- **H-01** · the seven rules of the method are necessary and sufficient for admitting rules to this repository · kind: claim · standing: hypothesis · measure: the count of rule lines on which the seven rules give an admission the owner judges wrong, and the count of pairs of rules whose verdicts coincide on every line examined · against: none · scope: the rules of the skills of this repository, as their provenance maps record them · refuter: one rule line the owner judges bad that passes all seven rules, one he judges good that the rules keep out, or two rules whose verdicts coincide on every line examined
- **H-02** · two readers who work independently from the definitions of the method give the same kind to the same rule · kind: claim · standing: hypothesis · measure: the number of thirty sampled rules to which both readers give the same kind · against: none · scope: the rules of this repository's four card corpora, read with their provenance lines by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort xhigh · refuter: fewer than 24 of the 30 rules get the same kind from both readers · threshold: 24 of 30 · runs: one pair of readers over one draw of thirty rules, registered in this history before it runs · test: own plugins/evidence-based-authoring/skills/testing-claims/evals/kind-agreement/protocol.md · result: none in this history yet; two runs made in the working history kept on the branch pre-squash-scientific-method gave 30 of 30 and 29 of 30, Cohen's kappa 1.00 and 0.94, and they entered this history in one commit with the hypothesis, so this history does not show that the hypothesis came first · bounds: of those two runs: two draws of thirty rules with no rule in both; readers that are two instances of one model, each in its own context with the same brief; the kinds of the method read with the reading rule of the protocol; rule lines shown without their records; sixty rules of about a thousand, none of the kind definition among them
