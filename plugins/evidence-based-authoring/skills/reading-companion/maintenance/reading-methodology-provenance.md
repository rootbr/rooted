# Provenance of the reading methodology

One line per source reference behind a claim of `references/reading-methodology.md`, keyed by its section (§1 … §13) and the claim, so that the methodology line and its provenance line together carry everything known; a clause of `SKILL.md` that claims an effect has its line here as well, keyed `SKILL.md` and its heading. Each line holds the citation as it stood in the methodology text and the claim it backs, then the tests and documents found for it, then its record in the format of `../../scientific-method/references/record-format.md`. A line whose claim waits for its test is its owner's decision, and its record names the claim as `H-<id>` in `pending-evidence.md`. `citations.json` holds the words and figures the lines quote from their sources, one entry per quote. The evidence mark (**high**, **moderate**, **low**, **heuristic**) stays on the claim in the methodology. The grouped bibliography the methodology carried closes this file.
