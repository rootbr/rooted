# Provenance of the method

One line per part of `references/method.md`, keyed `M-n`, and one per prescription of `SKILL.md` or `references/record-format.md` that the method states in brief or not at all, keyed `S-n`: the texts or the rules its formulation follows, then its record. The parts of the method are conventions the repository adopts, so their records are decisions and definitions; the texts below are the formulations those decisions follow. What would count as a test of the method stands in `maintenance/pending-evidence.md`.

How each text was seen: `first-hand` marks a text opened with a fetch tool that returns extracted passages, the quoted words being as that tool returned them; Mayo's §1.1 was read in the publisher's excerpt as extracted text; the selections from *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* were read in a course document whose text carries scanning errors; the PDFs cited under M-E, S-7 and S-8 were read as the text extracted from the downloaded file. `SEP` is the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/<slug>/. A thinker named with an SEP section is reported as that entry reports him; his own book was opened only where a line says so.

The quoted words of every line stand in `maintenance/method-citations.json`, one entry per quote with its source. `python3 -I maintenance/scripts/check_quotes.py --cache <dir> maintenance/method-citations.json`, run from the skill directory, says for each whether the words stand in the source; the sources that are not web pages, a Word document and PDFs, are converted to text first, as their entries say. Run over the texts fetched on 2026-11-21, the command finds every quote in its source. The SEP entries were opened that day in these revisions: scientific-method 2026-08-08, pseudo-science 2025-07-28, popper 2026-07-31, hume-moral 2018-08-20, scientific-objectivity 2020-10-30, operationalism 2019-09-17, physics-experiment 2023-06-02, scientific-knowledge-social 2025-01-24, induction-problem 2022-11-22, lakatos 2026-06-19, scientific-underdetermination 2023-04-04, naturalism 2020-03-31, abduction 2025-06-18, measurement-science 2020-08-07, and scientific-reproducibility as first published on 2018-12-03. A quote found in its source shows that the words are there; whether the passage carries what the line takes from it is a reader's judgement, and `research/2026-11-21_scientific-method-review.md` § Decision 3 lists where a reader found a departure.
