Each line holds the citation as it stood in the run-set text — author, short title, id or URL, section — then the claim it backs, then its record in the format of `../../scientific-method/references/record-format.md`; a line whose claim waits for its test names it as `H-<id>` in `pending-evidence.md`, and the quoted words stand in `citations.json` beside those of the rule cards. A rule card's sources stand on its line in `rule-cards-provenance.md`; the anchor tokens `R-xx`, `G-xx`, `claude-code#NNNNN` and `house convention` are decoded in `maintenance-map.md`.
