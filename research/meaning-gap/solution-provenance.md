# What the method of narrowing the meaning gap rests on

`solution.md` proposes ten practices by which an author narrows the gap between what he means and what his addressee takes. This map holds what the practices can lean on today: two claims that a published test supports, and eleven norms of trades in which a misunderstanding is costly. The twenty-one claims that no test supports stand in `pending-evidence.md`, twenty of them waiting for a test and one contested. A practice itself comes into force as the decision of whoever adopts it, and its record is written then, with its owner and its goal.

Both claims stood as hypotheses in the list before the search, with the measure, comparison, scope and refuting outcome they carry here. H-MG-01 restates the essay's own observation: a wording its author finds clear is expected to be understood, and is not. Its measure follows two published experiments whose results the session recalled when it worded the claim, one on ambiguous sentences and one on the tone of e-mail; both stand in `beside` as sources the claim was worded from, and its test is a later preregistered experiment, which the search found. H-MG-07 was worded after a recalled result, the study of likelihood terms in 24 countries, which stands in `beside` in the same way; its two tests are other experiments, which the search found. Of the first of them the session recalled that it measures how differently people read words of likelihood, and not its comparison of formats. At its registration H-MG-07 named two quantities, the spread of the readings and the share of them within the author's range, and its refuter asked for both to fail. Both tests measured the share and found it higher with the number, so the record carries the share alone; neither gives the spread between readers by arm, and that half of the claim waits in the list of hypotheses as H-MG-23.

Both supported claims reach the method's setting as a transfer: their tests ran on other people and other texts than a professional's finding for a client, and each record names the difference. A norm shows that a practice is accepted in its trade; that the practice works better is a claim of its own, recorded under its hypothesis.
