# Provenance of the appraisal methodology

One line per source reference behind a claim of the run set — `references/appraisal-methodology.md`, `SKILL.md` and `agents/debate/prompt.md` — keyed by the file and section, so that the run-set line and its provenance line together carry everything known. Each line holds the citation as it stood in the run-set text and the claim or method it backs, then the tests and documents found for it, then its record in the format of `../../scientific-method/references/record-format.md`. A line whose claim waits for its test is its owner's decision, and its record names the claim as `H-<id>` in `pending-evidence.md`; a line that states the claim of another line at a second place of the run set takes that line's record. `citations.json` holds the words and figures the lines quote from their sources, one entry per quote. The verification flags close this file: cautions on citations whose wording or figures are not confirmed first-hand.
