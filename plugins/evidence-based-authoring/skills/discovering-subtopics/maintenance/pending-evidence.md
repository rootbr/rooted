# Hypotheses of the discovering-subtopics skill

The claims of this skill that no test backs yet, keyed `H-<id of its line>` and named by that line's record in `methodology-provenance.md`. Each names its measure and the outcome that would refute it, and in `beside` the published sources found so far, with what they showed and how they bear on the claim in `beside-result` and the conditions they ran under in `beside-bounds`. A hypothesis whose test is planned as our own also names its threshold, its runs and the file that holds the test, and is committed here before the test runs. A hypothesis that a test settles keeps its line, with the result and the new standing.
