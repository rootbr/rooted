# Provenance of the researching-topics run set

One line per rule of `SKILL.md`, keyed by its section or by the gotcha id: the rule as the skill states it, the sources found for it with their locators, then its record in the format of `../../scientific-method/references/record-format.md`. A record says what the rule rests on: a norm with the document opened, the owner's decision, or a claim with its test, result and bounds. A rule whose claim waits for its test is its owner's decision, and its record names the claim as `H-<id>` in `pending-evidence.md`. `citations.json` holds the words and figures the lines quote from their sources, one entry per quote.
