# Hypotheses on the gap between the meaning an author intends and the meaning his addressee takes

A professional words a finding for an addressee of another organisation or trade, and the addressee takes another meaning from the words than the author put into them. The twenty-one claims below state that problem and the candidate ways of narrowing the gap, one comparison a claim. Twenty wait for their tests, and one, H-MG-19, is contested: of its two published tests one went against it and one both ways; `solution-provenance.md` holds the two claims of the same series that a published test supports, H-MG-01 and H-MG-07. Each claim was worded from an essay of a management consultancy on explaining findings to its clients and from recall of the literature, before a source was opened; the commit that adds a claim precedes the search for its test. The essay names three practices of its authors: wide exposure to clients, a colleague who reads a draft with fresh eyes, and the choice of the person who speaks to a client by how well the two have understood each other before. H-MG-04, H-MG-15 and H-MG-12 state the claims behind them.

Each claim names its measure, what it is compared against and the outcome that would refute it. `scope` is the place where a rule drawn from the claim would be applied. `beside` holds the published sources the search found (`research/2026-11-22_meaning-gap-evidence.md`, whose list of sources gives the address each was read at), with what each showed in `beside-result` and the conditions it ran under in `beside-bounds`. A source stands there for one of two reasons, which its clause names: it answers a neighbouring question, with another comparison, another measure or conditions too far from the claim's to be taken as a transfer; or it is the source the claim was worded from. A source in `beside` supports nothing. Fifteen claims carry a narrower measure or comparison than the first commit of this file gave them: where a field named two measures or two comparisons, the independent reader of the records asked for one, and the claim keeps the one its sentence names; none of the fifteen has a test, so no standing rests on the narrowing. H-MG-18 also names, at that reader's request, the property of an organisation its claim turns on, where its first wording compared any two organisations. H-MG-23 holds the half of H-MG-07 that its tests did not show: H-MG-07 named two quantities at its registration, the spread of the readings and the share of them within the author's range, the tests moved the share, and the narrowing of the spread waits here. `solution.md` gives the practice each claim stands behind and the comparison a firm can run on itself.
