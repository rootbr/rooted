# Maintaining handing-over-to-readers

Nothing under `maintenance/` or `evals/` is read or executed in a run of the skill. Paths are relative to the skill directory; `../../hooks/hooks.json` is the plugin's hook file.

## What changes together

- A rule: its numbered line in `references/rules.md`, its line in `maintenance/rules-provenance.md`, and the rules table of the research note `research/2026-10-21_reading-contract-evidence.md` (§Rules and data) at the repository root; a clause of `references/explaining.md` or `references/media.md` has its line in `maintenance/rules-provenance.md` and its row in `research/2026-11-07_report-forms-evidence.md` (§ Rules and data); a clause of `references/explainer.md`, and every clause that entered with the explanation page, has its line there and its row in `research/2026-11-14_explanation-page-evidence.md` (§ Rules and data); a clause that entered with the review of that page by its readers — the order of an explanation, the precision of a word, a number and a condition, the proof by one case, a measured comparison, the first part read before the rest — has its line there and its row in `research/2026-11-22_explainer-review-evidence.md` (§ Rules and data); a clause on the terms collected before the first sentence, a list and a marked block in the story, a term said again at its return, a compound condition, a worked case, the place and the labels of a picture, what a later part of an explanation holds, references that open in place, the voice by part with its pause and the tools of a selection, or the map of the page has its line there and its row in `research/2026-11-22_explanation-page-editing-evidence.md` (§ Rules and data); a clause that entered with the method of the meaning gap — the records read before the reader is asked, the long deliverable, the reader's words kept in his record, the record of an exchange, the opening sent to the reader first, the number beside a vague word, the cold reader's question for a thing the reader lacks, a finding in four parts — has its line there and its row in `research/2026-11-22_meaning-gap-evidence.md` (§ Rules and data). A clause that leaves the reference files moves to § Clauses that left of `maintenance/rules-provenance.md` with the decision that removed it, and its claim keeps its line in `maintenance/pending-evidence.md`. A rule number never moves, because `scripts/check_question.py`, `scripts/check_report.py` and the eval expectations name rules by number; a rule that leaves keeps its number as a gap. A held-back rule or option waits in `maintenance/pending-evidence.md` as a hypothesis keyed `H-held-…` and enters its reference file with its provenance line once a test backs it. A rule that is added or changed goes through `/scientific-method` first: its line and record are written, a claim with no test yet enters the list of hypotheses, and a record that claims support is read by the independent reader of that skill.
- A check: the script and its test module in `maintenance/tests/`. A script carries no check for a rule or option held out of the reference files, whose claim waits in `maintenance/pending-evidence.md` as `H-held-…`.
- A matched heading or word: the `WORDS` list of the script and the test that uses it; a user's lexicon file adds a language's alternatives without a change here. A key that leaves `WORDS` makes every lexicon still carrying it unreadable (exit 2), so its removal is announced to the users of the lexicon.
- The card form: `scripts/check_question.py` (`problems`, `GUIDANCE`), `maintenance/tests/test_check_question.py`, `../../hooks/hooks.json`, the card bullet of `SKILL.md` § 5, rule 18 of `references/rules.md`, and the eval `question-to-operator-with-default`.
- The report template: `references/report-template.md`, `check_story` and `LOOK_HEADING` of `scripts/check_report.py` with the `StoryForm` tests, `assets/page-body-example.html`, which draws the same report as a page, and the eval `operator-report-with-failing-build`.
- A delivery form: its row and section in `references/media.md`, the form bullet of `SKILL.md` § 5, the skill's description and its line in `maintenance/rules-provenance.md`.
- A kind of deliverable: its bullet in `SKILL.md` § 5, its reference file (`references/explainer.md` for the explanation), its place in the default row of `references/media.md`, its blocks in `references/page.md`, the skill's description, `README.md` and its lines in `maintenance/rules-provenance.md`.
- The ear rule of the story: `references/explaining.md` § The story, the worked report of `references/report-template.md`, `STORY_SENTENCE_WORDS` and `STORY_OPENING_SENTENCES` of `scripts/check_report.py` and the `StoryForm` tests.
- The page tooling: `references/media.md` § The page with voice, `references/page.md`, `scripts/voice_cues.py`, `scripts/build_page.py`, `scripts/check_page.py`, `assets/page-template.html` and `assets/page-body-example.html`, with `maintenance/tests/test_voice_cues.py`, `maintenance/tests/test_build_page.py`, `maintenance/tests/test_page_behaviour.py`, `maintenance/tests/test_check_page.py` and `maintenance/tests/fixtures/`. The body contract is stated once, in the docstring of `scripts/build_page.py`; `references/page.md` § The body and `assets/page-body-example.html` follow it. `scripts/build_page.py` loads the rule that ends a sentence from `scripts/voice_cues.py`, so the rule changes in that one script. A key that leaves `STRINGS` moves to `RETIRED_STRINGS`: a strings file still carrying it is read with a warning that names the key, and the key is dropped; a key in neither makes the file unreadable (exit 2). The behaviour tests run the built page in a headless Chrome (`$CHROME`, the macOS application, or `google-chrome`, `chromium` or `chromium-browser` on `PATH`) with the audio element stubbed, and are skipped where no such browser is found; a change to the voice by part, the pause, the tools of a selection, the comments, the map of the page, the references in place or the answer of the template is checked there. The list of terms (`--terms`) and the file of references (`--refs`, `--editor-url`) are stated in the docstring of `scripts/build_page.py`; `references/page.md` §§ The terms as data and References as data follow it, with the `Terms` and `References` tests of `maintenance/tests/test_build_page.py`. `scripts/build_page.py` imports `LOOK_HEADING` from `scripts/check_report.py`, so the heading of a report's closing list changes in both. The explanation check of `scripts/build_page.py` reads the classes `pseudo`, `questions` and `quiz` of a `figure` and the attribute `data-nav` of every figure; a class or a refusal changes in the script's docstring, in `references/page.md` § The blocks of an explanation, in `references/explainer.md` § Delivery and in the `Explanation` tests of `maintenance/tests/test_build_page.py`.
- The cold read: `SKILL.md` § 6, `references/explaining.md` § The cold read, the plugin's `../../agents/cold-reader.md`, `scripts/check_cold_read.py` with `maintenance/tests/test_check_cold_read.py`, and the measurement `evals/cold-read/` graded by `evals/grade_cold_read.py` (`maintenance/tests/test_grade_cold_read.py`). A change to the agent's instructions or to the questions is a new iteration under `evals/cold-read/runs/`, which keeps the instructions and the questions it ran with beside its answers; `evals/cold-read/plant.py` writes the planted reports and the blind copies. The retelling of a first part is stated once, in the docstring of `scripts/check_cold_read.py`: the `retell` key of the questions file, the `retelling` and `gaps` fields of the answer and the option `--steps`; the agent's definition, `SKILL.md` § 6 and `references/explaining.md` § The cold read follow it. The retelling is measured in `evals/retelling/` (its `README.md` states the texts, the two arms and the threshold), graded by `evals/grade_retelling.py` (`maintenance/tests/test_grade_retelling.py`); a change to the agent's instructions for the retelling is a new iteration under `evals/retelling/runs/`. The two readings of the rebuilt explanation page, by its four reviewers and by a newcomer, are fixed in `evals/explainer-reading/protocol.md`, and their counts go into `results.md` beside it.
- The reader record and the intake: `references/intake.md`, `SKILL.md` § 2, the `reader` commands of `scripts/records.py` with `maintenance/tests/test_records.py`, and the Intake lines of `maintenance/rules-provenance.md`. The boundary of a long deliverable is stated once, in `references/intake.md`; `SKILL.md` §§ 2 and 5 and `references/explaining.md` § The story follow it.
- The record of exchanges and the opening sent first: `references/intake.md` § After the deliverable, the opening bullet of `references/explaining.md` § The story, `SKILL.md` §§ 5 and 7, the `exchange` commands of `scripts/records.py` with their values of `--opening` and `--reply` and the `Exchange` tests, the events `exchange_add` and `exchange_note` of `scripts/feedback.py`, `README.md` § Records, and their two lines of `maintenance/rules-provenance.md`. The values are stated once, in the docstring of `scripts/records.py`.
- The vague word: its bullet in `references/explaining.md` § The story, the keys `VAGUE` and `NUMBER_WORDS` of `WORDS` in `scripts/check_report.py` with the `Vague` tests, the warning lists of `SKILL.md` § 6, and its line of `maintenance/rules-provenance.md`.
- The ledger and the gate: `SKILL.md` §§ 4 and 7, rules 3 and 25 of `references/rules.md`, the `ledger` commands of `scripts/records.py`, the gate in the hook mode of `scripts/check_question.py` with its tests, both entries of `../../hooks/hooks.json`, and the Gate lines of `maintenance/rules-provenance.md`. The floor and the count of candidates are house defaults held in `maintenance/pending-evidence.md`.
- The usage journal: `scripts/feedback.py` with its closed `SCHEMA`, the `journal()` call of every other script, `maintenance/tests/test_feedback.py`, `SKILL.md` § 8, `README.md` § Feedback and the issue form `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/handing-over-feedback.yml` at the repository root, whose file name the summary's address carries. A script records an event or a field only after `SCHEMA` holds it: a line outside the schema is dropped.
- A source: a reference file carries the rule and its numbers alone; the sources with their locators, the tests and documents found for the rule and the record of what it rests on stand on the rule's line in `maintenance/rules-provenance.md`, and rule g of the repository's `check-skill-layout.py` rejects a citation bracket, an eval tag or a fetch marker in a run-set file. The repository `README.md` §Handing-over-to-readers sources lists the first-hand academic and standard sources.

## Tests

From the repository root:

```
python3 -m unittest discover -s plugins/evidence-based-authoring/skills/handing-over-to-readers/maintenance/tests -p 'test_*.py'
```

The test fixtures are English reports and cards; the `Lexicon` cases add a heading or a word through a lexicon file and resolve the default lexicon under a temporary `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, never the user's own; the `--hook` tests run the script as the hook runs it; every test module points `HANDOVER_FEEDBACK_DIR` at a temporary directory, never the user's own journal.

## Evals

`evals/evals.json` holds the six evals in skill-creator's eval format: the prompt, the fixture files under `evals/fixtures/`, the expected output and the expectations. A run writes `<iteration>/eval-*/{with_skill,without_skill}/run-*/outputs/`, and `python3 evals/grade.py <iteration>` writes each run's `grading.json` from the expectations of `evals.json`, matched by the eval directory's name. A run's `benchmark.md` states its language, its baseline and every correction made to the grader after its first grading. Re-grading `evals/runs/iteration-2` reproduces the pass rates of its `benchmark.md`; a changed fixture or prompt is a new iteration. A report eval is graded by `story_facts` of `evals/grade.py` plus the facts of its own fixture; an expectation tag `E<n>` in `maintenance/rules-provenance.md` counts the expectations of `evals.json` in order.

## Layout

- `maintenance/rules-provenance.md` — one line per shipped rule, per "What the research adds" bullet and per clause of the reference files: its sources with locators, the formulation and the caveat, the tests and documents found for it, then its record in the format of `../../scientific-method/references/record-format.md`: a norm with the document opened, a definition, the owner's decision, or a claim with its test, result and bounds.
- `maintenance/pending-evidence.md` — the hypotheses of the skill: the claim behind each line whose record is its owner's decision, keyed `H-<id of the line>`, four claims held out of the reference files, keyed `H-held-…`, and the claim of each clause that left; each with its measure, its refuting outcome and the published sources found beside it.
- `maintenance/citations.json` — the words and figures the two files quote from their sources, one entry per quote with its source; an entry whose source is a PDF names the conversion to text in its `note`. From the repository root, `python3 -I plugins/evidence-based-authoring/skills/scientific-method/scripts/check_record.py --root . --require` on the two files checks that every line carries a well-formed record, and `python3 -I plugins/evidence-based-authoring/skills/scientific-method/maintenance/scripts/check_quotes.py --cache <dir>` on `citations.json` that every quoted fragment stands in its source.
- `maintenance/tests/` — the unit tests of the scripts, the hook mode included; `maintenance/tests/fixtures/` holds the files the page tests read.

Who reads or runs every other file of the skill, and when: `README.md` §Layout.
