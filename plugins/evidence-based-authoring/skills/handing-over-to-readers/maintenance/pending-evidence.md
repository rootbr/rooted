# Pending evidence of the reading contract

The rules and clauses of the operator's list that no opened `direct` or `transfer` source backs, in two groups: those held out of `references/rules.md` — a whole rule leaves a numbering gap — and those that ship on the committed eval run alone as `practice`. Each entry names what the backing search found and did not find and what would admit it; the research note `research/2026-10-21_reading-contract-evidence.md` § Rules and data holds the search result per rule. A script carries no check for an entry of the first group.

## Held out of `references/rules.md`

- **Rule 10, separate passes.** Text: "Each filter is applied to the whole list as its own pass." Found: nothing opened — Basili et al. 1996, doi:10.1007/BF00368702, gave metadata only; Ciolkowski 2009 was rate-limited, and its relay aggregate says perspective-based reading does not beat a checklist; the lead DOI for Porter, Votta & Basili 1995 resolves to another paper. Admits it: Basili et al. 1996 and Ciolkowski 2009 opened, and the correct DOI of Porter, Votta & Basili 1995. The definitional sentence of rule 10 ships, and `SKILL.md` step 3 runs rules 3–9 over the list without claiming that separate passes find more.
- **Rule 17, the reason clause.** Text: "The decisive reason stands inside the question, not as a separate justification beside it." Found: none. Admits it: a reading study comparing a decision question with the reason built into it against the same question followed by a separate justification.
- **Rule 23, the heading by content.** Text: "The heading names its content." Found: the `D-01` card says nothing about headings; Watanabe et al. 2026, arXiv:2602.17084, measures structure density, not heading wording (weak). Admits it: a rule card that covers heading wording, or an opened reader study of it.
- **Rule 26.** Text: "Every instruction of the list's owner is recorded with its quote, so that the rules can be rebuilt after the session." Found: Falessi et al. 2013, doi:10.1145/2491509.2491515, is weak and does not test quoting; the lead Mäder & Egyed 2015, doi:10.1007/s10664-014-9314-z, is not opened. Admits it: Mäder & Egyed 2015 opened, or one controlled study showing that traces from the work back to the instruction's source improve later work.
- **Rule 27.** Text: "After every series of edits the list is run through checks 3–9 again: items that passed the first run fall on the second." Found: nothing opened; Biffl, Freimut & Laitenberger 2001 is relay (a reinspection's net gain is positive but lower than the first inspection's). Admits it: one opened reinspection study — Biffl et al. 2001, or Biffl & Gutjahr 2002 in Empirical Software Engineering. `SKILL.md` step 5 keeps the run of the check scripts on the final text, which rests on the scripts and on eval client-question-list E1, operator-report-with-failing-build E1 and question-to-operator-with-default E1.
- **No narrative of the work, no retelling of the diff.** Text: "Narrative of the work and a retelling of what the diff shows are left out: the reader cannot check the first and sees the second himself." Found: no source compares a narration of the steps with a statement of the result; Pirouzkhah et al. 2026, arXiv:2602.14611, bounds the diff clause the other way — PRs whose description explains the code changes are 12–20 % more likely to be merged. Admits it: a reader study comparing a description that narrates the steps or restates the diff with one that states the result, the purpose and the evidence, measured on reviewer accuracy or time. The shipped text states what a report carries, and `scripts/check_report.py` holds no narrative check.

## Shipped on the eval run alone

- **Rule 2, the two-field format.** Found: Rao & Daumé III 2018 backs the selection criterion, not the format; Jacobson et al. 2017 is weak. Exercised by: eval question-to-operator-with-default E1, client-question-list E1. Admits it: a controlled reader study that compares a question carrying the known fact and the decision it changes against the bare question, measured on answer accuracy, time or follow-up rounds; or Howard 1966 opened first-hand.
- **Rule 6.** Found: Horvitz 1999 and Liu et al. 2026, both weak. Exercised by: eval client-question-list E7, question-to-operator-with-default E3–E5. Admits it: a study of delegated work comparing reversible, low-cost decisions taken by default and logged against the same decisions escalated, measured on the principal's time and the error rate; or the irreversibility premise opened first-hand (Arrow & Fisher 1974 or Henry 1974).
- **Rule 7.** Found: no source tests it; the clause follows from the expected-value definition that backs rule 4, and the search named no study for it. Exercised by: eval client-question-list E5.
- **Rule 8.** Found: Galesic & Bosnjak 2009, weak; Schwarz, Strack & Mai 1991 not opened. Exercised by: eval client-question-list E6. Admits it: Schwarz, Strack & Mai 1991 opened at its correct DOI, or a study of duplicated items in a question list measured on answer consistency or time.
- **Rule 9.** Found: nothing opened; three leads blocked. Exercised by: eval client-question-list E7, E8. Admits it: the full text or abstract of Nisbett, Zukier & Lemley 1981, Rey 2012 or Sundararajan & Adesope 2020 opened; each is a `transfer` at best, so two are needed.

- **Rule 18, the typed answer.** Found: none for the typed answer itself; the read-back rests on Cardosi 1993. Exercised by: eval question-to-operator-with-default E4. Admits it: the search named no study for it; Mosier et al. 1998, doi:10.1207/s15327108ijap0801_3, and Johnson & Goldstein 2003, doi:10.1126/science.1091721, the leads it names for the pull of a recommendation or default, are not opened.

Admits it: the DITA 1.3 conditional-processing section opened at its post-migration URL, a standard for filtering one source per audience, and the text of Byers 2004, doi:10.1109/MSECP.2004.1281241, for the transfer that a delivered source file leaks hidden internal content.
- **Rule 25.** Found: Falessi et al. 2013 and Nogueira et al. 2026, both weak. Exercised by: eval client-question-list E8, question-to-operator-with-default E5. Admits it: one opened controlled study showing that recorded decisions with their rationale improve later work, or the rationale clause of ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010.
