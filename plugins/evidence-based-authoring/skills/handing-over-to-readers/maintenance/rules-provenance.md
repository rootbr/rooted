# Provenance of the handing-over-to-readers rules

One line per shipped rule of `references/rules.md`, keyed `R-n` by the operator's number, one per bullet of its "What the research adds", and one per clause of `references/explaining.md`, `references/explainer.md`, `references/media.md` and `references/page.md`, keyed `Explaining:`, `Explainer:`, `Media:` and `Page:`; the lines keyed `Intake:`, `Gate:` and `Feedback:` cover `references/intake.md`, the ledger with its gate and the usage journal. The lines on the explanation page cite the research note `research/2026-11-14_explanation-page-evidence.md` by section. The clause lines cite the research note `research/2026-11-07_report-forms-evidence.md` by section: § The readings holds what the operator said at each of his seven readings of report forms (Reading 1 … Reading 7), § Measurements the counts taken on the texts, § What the literature adds the opened sources. Fields: the sources with their locators and the pointer to the quoted fragment · the formulation · the evidence class, the fit and whether the text was opened · the caveat. A source is `first-hand` when its text (abstract, HTML or full text, as the pointer says) was opened; a figure seen only in a search summary is `relay` and carries no rule. `README n` is entry n of the repository `README.md` §References, verified first-hand by the output-style programme. Classes and fit, recorded per rule in the research note `research/2026-10-21_reading-contract-evidence.md` § Rules and data: `research` (an opened `direct` or `transfer` study; a `weak` one is named only as consistent), `standard`, `practice` (the committed eval run, by eval name and expectation number, or the operator's readings, by reading number; listed in `maintenance/pending-evidence.md` with the study it needs), `definition`, `card`. The formulation of every rule is the operator's list of 27 rules; a second formulation is named where one exists.
