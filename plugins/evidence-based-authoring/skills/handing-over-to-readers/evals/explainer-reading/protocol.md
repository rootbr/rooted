# Two readings of the rebuilt explanation page

The explanation page the genre is drawn from was rebuilt in the order of `../../references/explainer.md`: the mechanism whole in its first part, the tables after it. Two readings check that page. The page and the records of the meetings stay with the repository's owner; this file fixes what is counted, and `results.md` beside it takes the counts.

## The four reviewers read it again

- **Who**: the four readers who reviewed the first build.
- **What is counted**: the questions they ask at the review whose answer the page already holds, counted from the record of the meeting as the first count was; and whether they retell the steps of the algorithm from the page without opening the code.
- **Threshold**: fewer than six such questions, and a retelling without the code.
- **What it shows**: six or more such questions, or readers who cannot retell the algorithm, go against the order. A lower count supports nothing: the readers have read the code since the first review.

## A newcomer reads it

- **Who**: the first engineer who joins the team and has read neither the code of the mechanism nor the first build.
- **Before the reading**: the steps of the pseudocode of the page's first part are numbered, and their count goes into `results.md` with its date. The page stays as it is from that line to the reading.
- **What he gets**: the rebuilt page alone, without its author. He reads it once, the page is closed, and he writes the algorithm down by its steps.
- **What is counted**: a step of the pseudocode counts as retold when a sentence of his text says what the step does and stands after the sentence of the step before it. A person who did not write the page makes the count from the written retelling. The newcomer says whether he opened the code before he wrote.
- **Threshold**: at least four fifths of the steps, without the code.
- **What it shows**: a lower share, or the code opened, goes against the claim that the page reads without its author and without the code. One reader with no second page beside him says nothing on whether this order is better than the first.

## The record

`results.md` takes one line a reading: the date, which reading, the counts, who counted. The newcomer's line gives the steps by their numbers, each retold or missed, so the share can be counted again from the line. Nothing of the page, the mechanism or the readers' names goes into it.
