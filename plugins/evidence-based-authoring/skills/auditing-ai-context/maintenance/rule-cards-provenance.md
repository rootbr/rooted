# Rule-cards provenance map

Out-of-runtime maintainer record. The audit workflow does **not** pass this file to sub-agents — cards carry the rule and its numbers and no citation; this map carries the full citation with its locator, the origin, and the maintenance notes. Round-trip rule: a card plus its line here reconstruct everything known about the rule.

One line per card: `rule_id · source · origin-file · notes` (ownership / non-default `applies_to_target` / evidence caveat, where notable). `group` is derivable from `rule_id`; `confidence`/evidence strength is the caveat in the notes. The `origin` g1–g8 group files were removed once carded — git history preserves them; the cards are now canonical. G10 and G11 have no group file: their origin is the section named on each line inside `research/2026-08-02_output-style-evidence.md`, and that note's three `Verification —` sections hold the quotes and numbers those cards copy.
